Quiet or Unheard?

Team E Territories 5 · 7 · 10 For: UN analysts
Our question

How might we help UN analysts tell a quiet place from an unheard one, so early warning rests on an honest picture of what is and isn't known?

Concept

Silence as a data point

Most tools work with the data you already have. This one measures the data you don't: it compares the reporting a place normally gets with what is arriving now, checks any silence against independent signals, and keeps that gap attached to the analysis all the way up to the envoy.

No trust scores No verdicts A person makes the call
Scenario

Two districts look the same on the map

A district in Cameroon's Northwest normally produces about 15 reports a month, including local-language community radio. This month there are 2, both in English, both from outlets in the capital. Satellite imagery shows newly burned farmland.

On a conflict map, few reports reads as calm. Is this district quiet, or is no one hearing it?

Why this could work

Grounded in DPPA's work, and already proven elsewhere

From our research and the UN

  • DPPA's tools avoid "embedding analysis." The user adds the judgment, so this surfaces gaps and never concludes.
  • GeoGuard's conflict data had no category for farmer-herder clashes, so they were invisible on the map.
  • DPPA's own chain runs inputs → language → analysis → outputs. Doubt tends to drop off as a claim moves up.
  • Conflict event data comes mostly from news, which under-reports rural and local-language areas.

Already done in other fields

  • Zero reporting in disease surveillance: clinics report "no cases," so silence is never mistaken for health.
  • Ships "going dark": switched-off trackers hid about 6% of global fishing (source).
  • Internet shutdown monitors (IODA, #KeepItOn) treat a region going silent as a warning sign.
  • El Fasher, 2025: under a blackout, satellite imagery documented what reporting couldn't.

  1. What counts as "normal" coverage for a place, and who gets to decide?

  3. How do we explain the difference from concepts that flag what AI loses when summarizing voices it collected? We track what was never collected.
